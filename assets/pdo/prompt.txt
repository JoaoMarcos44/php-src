PR title: Reset the prefetched row when closing a PDO SQLite cursor

Validation and documentation: The regression fails before and passes after the patch on PHP 8.7.0-dev. See findings/pdo_sqlite/PR.md and findings/pdo_sqlite/patch/rationale.md.
